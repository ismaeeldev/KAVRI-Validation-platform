# Tester Workspace Guide

This guide describes the secure mobile-first validation experience for Testers.

## 1. Account Onboarding
1. **Accept Invitation**:
   - Open the secure invitation link shared by the platform owner (`/invite/[token]`).
   - Confirm your display name and set a secure password.
   - Click `Activate Account`.
2. **Log In**:
   - Access the login view at `/login`.
   - Enter your email and password to access the portal.

## 2. Validation Briefs & Acknowledgement
1. **Tester Overview**:
   - Upon logging in, you will be redirected to the mobile-first dashboard `/tester`.
   - View your active testing cards list (displays public alias specs, revision codes, and due dates).
2. **View Brief & Acknowledge**:
   - Click `View Brief` on an active card.
   - Read the detailed validation instructions.
   - Click the primary action button: `Acknowledge Brief`.
   - Once clicked, the status changes to `acknowledged` to confirm validation cycles have begun.
